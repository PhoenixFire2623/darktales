Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Who uses this app

Dark Tales Uploader is a personal tool operated solely by the owner of the Dark Tales Daily channel. The only account it ever connects to is the owner's own YouTube channel and Instagram account.

What data it accesses

  • YouTube: only the youtube.upload permission, used to upload videos the owner created to the owner's channel. It does not read viewers' data, comments, subscribers or analytics.
  • Instagram: only permission to publish Reels to the owner's own professional account.

How data is stored

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's personal computer. No data is sent to any server operated by this app, sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Google API Services

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. This app also uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at Google Account permissions and in Instagram under Settings → Apps and websites. Revoking access stops all uploads; locally stored tokens are deleted by the owner.

Contact

Questions can be sent through the Dark Tales Daily YouTube channel's About page.
